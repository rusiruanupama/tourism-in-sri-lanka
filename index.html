TRAVEL INFORMATION

Useful before you go.

Quick guidance for planning, moving around and staying prepared.

01

Best time to visit

Sri Lanka can be travelled year-round, but the best coast depends on the season. Check the regional weather before choosing your route.

02

Getting around

Trains, buses, tuk-tuks, taxis and private vehicles are common options. Travel time can be longer than the map distance suggests.

03

Respect & culture

Dress appropriately at religious sites, follow photography rules and respect local customs and communities.

04

Money & planning

Keep some local currency available and confirm current attraction fees, transport fares and accommodation prices before booking.
