WHEN TO VISIT

Sri Lanka Season Guide

Weather varies across the island, so the best coast or region depends on the time of year.

🌤️

December – February

Northeast monsoon period. Conditions can differ significantly between regions.

🌦️

March – April

First inter-monsoon period, with changing weather patterns around the island.

🌧️

May – September

Southwest monsoon period, particularly important when planning coastal travel.

⛈️

October – November

Second inter-monsoon period with rainfall possible across many areas.
